# Context Switching

TLDR: Switching between tasks incurs a cognitive cost that persists for 10–20 minutes. KBS's session close ritual is designed to externalize task state, reducing the residue that makes context switching expensive.

## Key Claims
| Claim | Confidence | Conditions | Source |
|-------|------------|------------|--------|
| Context switching degrades performance | HIGH | Evidence from psychology (Meyer, Rubinstein) | External: APA research |
| Session close reduces switching cost | MEDIUM | Close is thorough and timely | [[success-validation]] |
| Batch processing reduces switching | MEDIUM | Weekly ingestion is done in one block | [[weekly-ingestion]] |
