# Attention Residue

TLDR: When you switch tasks, part of your attention remains on the previous task — this is "attention residue." It is the primary reason deep work requires long unbroken blocks. KBS captures incomplete thoughts to free attention.

## Key Claims
| Claim | Confidence | Conditions | Source |
|-------|------------|------------|--------|
| Attention residue persists 10–20 min | HIGH | Evidence from Sophie Leroy's research | External: Leroy 2009 |
| Externalizing thoughts reduces residue | MEDIUM | Capture is trusted and will be processed | [[rapid-capture]] |
