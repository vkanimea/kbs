# Session Management

TLDR: The disciplined practice of opening and closing focused work sessions is the single most important habit for compounding knowledge. A session without a close is a session without learning.

## Key Claims
| Claim | Confidence | Conditions | Source |
|-------|------------|------------|--------|
| Daily session close compounds knowledge | HIGH | Owner runs close within 24h of session | [[success-validation]] |
| Skipped closes cause knowledge decay | HIGH | Measured over 30+ day periods | [[failure-learning]] |
| Close rituals take 2–5 minutes | MEDIUM | Level 0–1 with <20 INBOX entries | [[daily-review]] |
