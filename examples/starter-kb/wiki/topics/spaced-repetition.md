# Spaced Repetition

TLDR: The scientific basis for KBS's query-and-compound cycle. Reviewing knowledge at increasing intervals strengthens retention. The daily query + monthly health check approximate a spaced repetition schedule for insights, not flashcards.

## Key Claims
| Claim | Confidence | Conditions | Source |
|-------|------------|------------|--------|
| Spaced review improves long-term retention | HIGH | Evidence from cognitive psychology (Ebbinghaus, Wozniak) | External: SuperMemo research |
| KBS queries approximate spaced repetition | MEDIUM | Owner queries diverse topics, not just recent ones | [[daily-review]] |
