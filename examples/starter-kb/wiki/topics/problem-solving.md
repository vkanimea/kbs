# Problem Solving

TLDR: Structured problem-solving separates observation from hypothesis, and solution from conditions. The KBS pattern forces explicitness that prevents overconfident claims.

| Claim | Confidence | Conditions | Source |

| Solutions without conditions are dangerous | HIGH | Applied to technical and non-technical domains | [[failure-learning]] |
| Problem:solution ratio ~1:1 indicates healthy KB | MEDIUM | Measured over 90-day windows | [[monthly-health-check]] |
